Evidence Explorer

Unlike black-box navigation apps that assign a singular, unverifiable safety score to an entire trip, SafePath breaks routes down into discrete street segments. Inspect exactly which data sources corroborate each segment, when they were last refreshed, and what critical information is missing.

COMMUNITY CONTRIBUTIONS

Street Observations & Environmental Reports

Community reports provide localized context regarding broken streetlights, sidewalk blockages, and visibility concerns.

Responsible Reporting Standards

To protect individual privacy and prevent harassment, SafePath strictly regulates report submissions:

  • Never include personally identifiable information, phone numbers, or private home addresses.
  • Report physical infrastructure conditions (e.g. lighting, sidewalk obstruction), not individuals.
  • All reports pass through a 4-state moderation pipeline (pending, approved, rejected, flagged).
  • One anonymous report does not prove an area is dangerous; corroboration is required.
!
Reports submitted here are NOT monitored by police or emergency dispatchers.
RESEARCH METHODOLOGY & CIVIC DATA INTEGRITY

How SafePath Treats Safety, Uncertainty & Data

1. Uncertainty is explicitly exposed

Rather than interpolating or guessing safety scores for unmapped roads, SafePath marks them as Insufficient Information. You are always informed when a route has blind spots.

2. Separation of risk and evidence

Evidence quality (how much data is collected) is maintained as an independent metric from estimated environmental risk. High documentation does not imply absolute safety.
